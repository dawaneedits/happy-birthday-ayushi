AYUSHI BIRTHDAY WEBSITE 💜

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP folder.
2. Open the extracted Ayushi-Birthday-NEAT folder.
3. Double-click index.html (open it with Chrome).
4. Click "OPEN YOUR SURPRISE" to reveal the birthday page.
5. Background music starts after the click. Use the Music ON/OFF button.

YOUR FOLDER
- index.html                 Website page
- music/birthday.mp3         Background music
- photos/                    Main photo + 10 gallery photo slots

IMPORTANT PHOTO NOTE
The photos folder currently contains purple placeholder graphics, not Ayushi's real photos.
Replace them with your actual photos:
- Main photo: photos/friend.jpg
- Gallery: photos/photo1.jpg through photos/photo10.jpg

After adding your photos, update the image paths in index.html:
- Main image: photos/friend.jpg
- Gallery images: photos/photo1.jpg ... photos/photo10.jpg

Keep the folder structure unchanged. If you send the actual 10 photos in this chat,
they can be placed into the folder for you.
